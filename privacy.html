ZikrNama - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

ZikrNama is a free tasbeeh (dhikr counter) app built on one promise: it never shows you an ad, never loses your count, and never asks who you are. This policy explains, plainly, how the app treats your data.

What we collect: nothing

ZikrNama does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting services, and no user accounts. The Android app does not even request internet access - its only system permission is vibration, used for haptic feedback while counting.

Your counts stay on your device

Your dhikr counts, history, streaks, and settings are stored only in a local database on your own device. They are never uploaded anywhere. Neither the developer nor any third party can see them.

Export and deletion

You can export all of your data at any time from Settings (as JSON or CSV), and you can permanently erase everything with Settings > Delete everything, which removes all data from the app's storage on your device. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data.

Children

ZikrNama is suitable for general audiences. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future feature ever changes any of the above (for example, an optional online feature), this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the change will be clearly described in the app's release notes. The core promise - no ads, no tracking, no accounts - will not change.
